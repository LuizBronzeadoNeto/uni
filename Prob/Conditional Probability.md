### Theorem 2.3.1 (intersection of two events) 
For any events A with B positive probabilities:
$$P(A \cap B) = P(B)P(A|B) = P(A)P(B|A)$$
### Bayes' rule
$$P(A|B)=\frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}$$
This follows immediately from theorem 2.3.1
### Theorem 2.3.6 (Law of total probability)
Let $A_1, A_2,...,A_n$ be a partition of sample space $S$ with $P(A_i)>0$, then:$$P(B)= \sum_{i=1}^n P(B|A_i)P(A_i)$$
